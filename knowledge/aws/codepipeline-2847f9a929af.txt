AWS CodePipeline
What is AWS CodePipeline?
AWS CodePipeline is a continuous delivery service you can use to model, visualize, and automate
        the steps required to release your software. You can quickly model and configure the
        different stages of a software release process. CodePipeline automates the steps required to
        release your software changes continuously. For information about pricing for CodePipeline, see
